# Code of Conduct

Book in Cambodia is a community project for readers, booksellers, librarians, writers and volunteers. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their language, background or experience.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

**Do**

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to GitHub or writing in their second language.
- Respect different opinions, and focus on what's best for readers in Cambodia.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility and apologize when you make a mistake.

**Don't**

- Harass, insult or make personal or political attacks.
- Use sexualized language or imagery.
- Publish anyone's private information (phone numbers, addresses) without their permission. Store contact details are fine when the store shares them publicly.
- Use the project to advertise, spam, or list fake stores or pirated books.

## Scope

This applies in GitHub issues, pull requests and discussions, in the Keystatic editor, and in community spaces such as Telegram groups run for this project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code of conduct, email **hello@bookincambodia.org** or contact a maintainer privately. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban people from the project, depending on how serious the behavior is.
